Cladophora

Systematic Position

Division: Chlorophyta
Class: Ulvophyceae
Order: Cladophorales

Morphology

• Filamentous, branched multicellular thallus
• Cells contain reticulate chloroplasts

Salient Features

• Common in freshwater and marine habitats
• Forms dense green mats
• Indicator of nutrient-rich (eutrophic) conditions

Economic Importance

• Important primary producer in aquatic ecosystems
• Used in ecological monitoring of water quality
